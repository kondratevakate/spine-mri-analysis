Data, model inputs and decision rules

RSNA LumbarDISC central canal grades are normal/mild, moderate and severe. The rectangle marks the estimated 48 mm disc-level crop. Its color encodes the current decision. Its location stays fixed when thresholds change. It does not trace a lesion boundary.

Four lumbar classifiers use a ResNet-18 baseline, matched whole sagittal image, sagittal crop or paired sagittal/axial crops. The axial projection has uncertain cross-series correspondence. The fifth uses the frozen 2026 RSNA Knee ConvNeXt-T encoder with a three-class lumbar logistic head trained on 256 training studies. Each class score is a weighted arithmetic mean. A minimum applies to the score of the assigned class. Scores have not been clinically calibrated.

In threshold mode, select severe when its score meets the threshold. Otherwise select the larger of the two remaining scores. Highest-class mode takes the largest of all three. A deferred case counts as undetected for severe sensitivity and is excluded from decided-case accuracy.

Neighbors come from 299 separate validation studies. For each selected reference grade, the viewer ranks same-level sagittal crops by cosine similarity between standardized, frozen knee-encoder embeddings, then applies the decision-status filter. Ties use study order. Similarity is a visual descriptor, without physician validation. The internal test studies were previously inspected. All interactive cohort metrics are exploratory.
