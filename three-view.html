The green points are RSNA reference annotations in patient coordinates. The 3D surfaces come from TotalSegmentator total_mr at 3 mm. Vertebrae are represented as one structure; vertebral numbering is supplied by the dataset landmarks. Masks and contours need specialist review.

Sagittal images and the 3D model share the same DICOM geometry. Axial T2 is a separately acquired series selected using bilateral same-level landmarks. The axial image is shown for inspection; a registered axial plane has not been established on the 3D model.

The earlier automatic landmark at L4/L5 was displaced by about 31 mm from the reference. The older classification scores used automatically positioned crops and require re-evaluation after that localization error.
